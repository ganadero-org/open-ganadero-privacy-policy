### 1. INTRODUCTION

At **GANADERO.ORG**, we respect our users' privacy and recognize the importance of protecting information relating to livestock farmers and their farming operations.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we may collect, how we use it, with whom we may share it, how long we retain it, and what rights users have.

GANADERO is currently a technology project in the research, development, and testing phase. Our objective is to create digital tools that help Spanish livestock farmers manage their operations through mobile applications, a web portal, and artificial intelligence solutions.

Participation in the pilot program is free of charge and governed by our Pilot Program Terms and Conditions.

The experimental nature of the product does not reduce our legal data protection obligations.

### 3. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT

Depending on the features used, GANADERO may process the following categories of information.

**3.1. Registration and Account Data**

- First and last name, when provided.
- Email address.
- Password protected through secure storage mechanisms.
- Telephone number, when provided.
- Internal user identifier.
- Language and configuration preferences.
- Account creation date and account activity.

**3.2. Livestock Operation Information**

Users may enter information related to their activities, including:

- Name and identification of the livestock operation.
- Livestock identification numbers and codes.
- Farms, land plots, facilities, and locations.
- Animal groups, pastures, and livestock organization.
- Animal information, including identifiers, breed, sex, age, and parentage.
- Animal health, reproduction, and treatment records.
- Weights, movements, births, and other events.
- Photographs and documents related to the livestock operation.
- Task records, alerts, and activities
